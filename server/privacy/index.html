Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026. This English version is the binding version. In case of differences the English version applies.

In short

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and, as a backup, on our server under a random restore code – without your name.
  • If you use the optional world high-score list, we store only a made-up call sign, your score/wave and the time.
  • We collect game statistics (for example wave reached, cause of game over, tutorial progress) and an error log to balance the game and fix bugs. Some of them are linked to your random player number. We store no name, phone number or e-mail – we do not know who you are (pseudonymous data).
  • The game keeps a player profile under a random player number (progress, play time, device description) so we can help players and improve the game – without your real name.
  • We do not sell or share data. Our server is in Finland (EU).

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings, best results and the call sign you chose in your device's local storage. This data leaves your device only as described below. It is deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the game.

World high-score list and Nuts board (optional)

If you submit a result, our server stores:

  • your call sign (a made-up name, 2–16 letters, digits, spaces, "-" or "_" — please do not use your real name),
  • game mode, score, wave reached, lines and splats (high-score list), or continent (1–4) and level reached (Nuts board),
  • the time of the entry,
  • a random player ID created by the game on your device, used only to keep one best entry per player. It is not linked to your device, account or identity.

The call sign, score/level and time are publicly visible in the game. Together with the random player ID these entries are pseudonymous personal data. Entries are deleted after 24 months without a new result from the same player, or earlier if you ask us. Call signs are checked by a filter; entries that look like real names or contain insults are rejected or removed. You can report an entry to us by e-mail.

Anonymous round statistics

At the end of a round the game sends: wave reached, cause of game over, gold, lines, splats, score, medals, highest game speed, round duration, chosen hero, game mode and version, and the time. No call sign, no player ID and no other identifier is sent with these statistics. We use them only in aggregated form (for example, how many rounds end at which wave) to balance the game. Individual round records are deleted after 12 months at the latest.

Cloud backup of your progress

So that your progress is never lost (for example after changing your phone), the game backs up your local game data on our server: progress, best results, medals, settings and your made-up call sign. The backup is filed under a random restore code that only you see in the game (☁ Cloud save). We store only a fingerprint (hash) of the code, never the code itself, and no name, e-mail or device ID. With the code you can restore your progress on any device. We keep the last five backups per code; backups are deleted 24 months after the last update, or earlier if you ask us and send the code.

Anonymous error and usage log

To find and fix bugs, the game sends short technical notes to our server: error messages of the game, the wave and screen where you left or came back, the game version, a rough device description (operating system and browser version, screen size, memory class) and a random session number that is created anew every time the game starts and is never stored on your device. No call sign, no player ID, no advertising ID and no other identifier is sent. We use the log only in aggregated form. Entries are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

Player profile and player number

The game creates a random player number on your device (for example K7Q2-9HXM, shown in the settings under "Support info"). Under this number it regularly sends a player profile to our server: your made-up call sign, chosen hero and outfit, game language and version; your progress (current and best wave, checkpoint, stars, medals, squad level, missions, daily streak, number of invited friends, best Nuts levels); totals (splats, lines, waves, runs); play time and screen time (how long the game was open); number and cause of game overs; and a device description (operating system and version, device model, app or browser version, screen size, number of processor cores and memory class, device language and time zone). We also store when the profile was first and last received and how often the game was started.

The profile contains no real name, no e-mail, no advertising ID, no location and no IP address. It is pseudonymous personal data. Only the developer can view it, in a protected admin view; it is not shown to other players and not shared. We use it to see how far players get, to improve the game and to help you when you contact support. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the game and supporting players (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Profiles are deleted 24 months after the last update. You can object or ask for deletion at any time: send us your player number by e-mail.

Game events

Since version V594 the game sends short game events under your random player number: tutorial lessons and their duration, the certificate, boss hacks (offered, accepted, won, lost), settings changes (music, sound, language), purchases with in-game gold, use of specials, in-game offers, wave and cause of game over, restarts, loading time, low frame rate, and the install source (website, app store, home-screen shortcut). They contain no name, phone number, e-mail, advertising ID or location. We use them to see where players get stuck and to balance the game. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Events are deleted 24 months after the last update of the player profile; you can object or ask for deletion at any time by e-mailing us your player number.

Server, IP address, cookies

Our server is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in a data centre in Finland (EU), acting as our processor. To deliver a request your IP address is necessarily processed. We do not store IP addresses: for abuse protection (rate limiting) we use only a hash of the IP address combined with a random value that changes every day, kept in memory and never written to disk. We keep no access logs with IP addresses. We use no cookies.

No sale, no sharing, no tracking

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, show no ads and include no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Legal basis (EU/EEA)

Submitting a score is voluntary. We process the data described above on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a public leaderboard, balancing the game and protecting the service from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The call sign must be made up; if a real name is entered by mistake, contact us and we will delete the entry.

Your rights

You can ask for information about, correction or deletion of your entries and object to processing. Write to martjusha2021@gmail.com with your call sign and, if possible, the score and date. Because we store no identifying data, we may not be able to link anonymous round statistics to you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
